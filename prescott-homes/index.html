Why this order

  1. Quintero first. It’s on the north edge of town, so you hit it on the way in from Phoenix without backtracking.
  2. Idylwild next. It’s downtown, 18 minutes from Quintero.
  3. Banning Creek, then Lavalley. They’re 4 minutes apart on the south side of downtown, one off White Spar Rd.
  4. Quartz and Ponderosa Park last. They’re a minute apart in Ponderosa Park, the farthest south. Quartz and Ponderosa Park came from the agent’s list on Sep 26.

Pyrite went under contract on Sep 23, so it’s off the route. It’s still on the Homes and Compare tabs for reference. All six fit on Saturday if you keep visits to about 45 minutes, finishing around 5 PM. Move any to Sunday with its → Sun button. Sunset is about 6:25 PM.
