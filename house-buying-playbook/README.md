# The House Buying Playbook

A personal field guide to buying a house in 2026, in the same format as the FinOps and
Cloud Negotiations playbooks: 16 sections from budget and loans through agents, offers,
inspections, closing, second homes, Arizona and Prescott-area notes and ownership, plus three small in-browser calculators
(payment, points break-even, blended rate).

Single self-contained `index.html`, no build step, no tracking. Deep-link to a section
with `#N` (e.g. `#11` for second homes).

Figures are current as of September 2026 (Freddie Mac PMMS, FHFA 2026 loan limits,
2025 tax act). Not legal, tax or lending advice.
